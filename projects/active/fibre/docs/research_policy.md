# FIBRE research policy

**Definition:** FIBRE models catalytic compatibility with an interaction atlas: heterogeneous molecular representations define local reaction-demand / enzyme-capability coordinates, local experts define chart-specific interaction forms, and a partition of unity glues them into one globally defined interaction.

1. A broad raw-input chart must cover every valid reaction/enzyme pair.
2. Optional molecular views create additional charts; their absence never removes a prediction.
3. Different representations may have different dimensions and need not be metrically aligned.
4. Experts represent local descriptions of the same catalytic interaction, not additive residuals on top of a privileged model.
5. Chart applicability is biochemical and information-dependent, not dataset-ID membership.
6. TPS is a family chart that may apply inside a large candidate universe.
7. Sparse verified enzyme–reaction pairs supervise local interaction forms, chart applicability and overlap consistency.
8. New verified experiments may enter as provenance-bound finite-rank empirical interaction updates without retraining molecular encoders.
9. Only the reproduction profile may justify method-performance claims; application/full-data results are operational results.
10. Historical product-manifold/correspondence constructions and the rejected global bilinear residual are not current FIBRE ontology.
