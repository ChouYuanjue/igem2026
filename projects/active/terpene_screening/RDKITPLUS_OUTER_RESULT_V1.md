# RDKit+ clean outer confirmation v1

This report freezes the first full-universe confirmation of the reaction-side RDKit+ representation selected exclusively on clean2023 internal folds. The outer protocol and both evaluated variants were committed before outer-label reveal (`d44d2b9`, `cfc9a9a`). The new branch starts exactly identical to the original 2115-d base reaction tower: on 512 real registered reactions the zero-initialized hidden residual had max/mean embedding difference 0.0 before train-only continuation. Held-out outer entities never enter the continuation loss candidate pool.

## Main result

The clearest gain is the CAGE-uncovered reaction-cold/full-universe setting: 744 unseen reactions are each searched against 185,918 proteins while the test proteins remain train-seen. Relative to the previously frozen novelty model, RDKit+ plus the already-frozen novelty recipe improves Hit@1 from 3.63% to 6.32%, Hit@10 from 13.58% to 16.94%, Hit@50 from 26.61% to 31.32%, MRR from 0.0702 to 0.0991, MAP from 0.0536 to 0.0727, AUROC from 0.9199 to 0.9288, and median best-positive rank from 418.5 to 275.5. Against the earlier nested clean model, Hit@10 and Hit@50 rise by 6.72 and 7.12 percentage points. Query-paired 20,000-sample bootstrap intervals versus the frozen novelty model exclude zero for Hit@1/10/50, MRR, MAP, AUROC and NDCG@10.

The benefit survives strict double-cold evaluation but becomes smaller. On the ReactZyme reaction-projected double-cold cell, RDKit+ plus frozen novelty raises Hit@50 from 8.51% to 10.33%, MRR from 0.00968 to 0.01329, MAP from 0.00965 to 0.01302 and AUROC from 0.8801 to 0.8891; Hit@10 rises only from 3.34% to 3.95% and its paired bootstrap interval reaches zero, so no robust early-Top10 improvement is claimed. On the post-2020 creation-date double-cold cell, Hit@10 rises from 4.32% to 5.19%, Hit@50 from 10.62% to 12.10%, MRR from 0.01860 to 0.02153 and AUROC from 0.91835 to 0.92497. Paired bootstrap supports Hit@10/50, MRR, AUROC and NDCG@10 there, while MAP and Hit@1 remain inconclusive. This temporal cell is creation-date extrapolation, not a fully frozen historical database snapshot.

## Remaining failure mode

Very low reaction similarity remains the main unsolved case. In the reaction-cold cell, the `<0.3` DRFP-similarity bucket contains only 26 queries, so its new 3.85% Hit@1/10/50 must be treated as exploratory rather than stable evidence. In ReactZyme strict double-cold, the `<0.3` bucket still has Hit@10=0 after RDKit+, although median best-positive rank improves from 5,658.5 to 4,840 and AUROC from 0.805 to 0.813. In post-2020 double-cold, `<0.3` Hit@10 also remains zero while median rank improves from 12,136 to 9,333 and AUROC from 0.816 to 0.846. Therefore the representation materially improves global ordering, but extreme reaction OOD still needs another mechanism to convert better coarse ranking into useful Top-K recall.

## Decision

RDKit+ is promoted as the reaction-side representation for further clean research because it is the first intervention in this iteration with large, reproducible internal gains and clear full-universe outer benefit without association leakage. No additional RDKit+ hyperparameter will be selected from these outer results. Further reaction-side work should target the `<0.3` similarity regime with methods frozen on internal train/dev only (for example stronger reaction representation or a train-only reranker), while the next major orthogonal axis is protein representation for protein-cold/E2R using EnzGFM-style enzyme-specific foundation embeddings. CAGE-comparable tuning remains secondary unless these general representation improvements transfer naturally.
