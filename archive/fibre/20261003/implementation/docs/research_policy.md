# FIBRE research policy

**Definition:** FIBRE models a declared task-level reaction--enzyme catalytic compatibility with an interaction atlas. Heterogeneous molecular representations define local reaction-demand / enzyme-capability coordinates, and local charts estimate the same working compatibility. Physical activity is condition-dependent \(A(r,e;c)\); the current \(K^\star(r,e)\) is not asserted to be a universal context-free activity law.

1. A broad raw-input chart must cover every valid reaction/enzyme pair.
2. Optional molecular views create additional charts; their absence never removes a prediction.
3. Different representations may have different dimensions and need not be metrically aligned.
4. Experts represent local descriptions of the same task-level catalytic compatibility, not additive residuals on top of a privileged model.
5. Chart applicability is biochemical and information-dependent, not dataset-ID membership.
6. TPS is a family chart that may apply inside a large candidate universe.
7. Sparse verified enzyme--reaction pairs supervise local interaction forms, chart applicability and overlap consistency; unknown pairs are not automatically biological negatives.
8. The frozen realization may use direction-conditioned partitions for R2E and E2R while sharing local pair scores. Pointwise equality of the two directional readouts is not claimed unless explicitly verified.
9. New verified experiments may enter as provenance-bound bounded finite-rank interaction updates without retraining molecular encoders. Chart-local update primitives are implemented; cross-chart application orchestration must be claimed separately.
10. Only the reproduction profile may justify method-performance claims; application/full-data results are operational results.
11. Context-scoped observations retain assay provenance. A negative observation under one condition is not a permanent global negative edge.
12. Historical product-manifold/correspondence constructions and the rejected global bilinear residual are derivational/negative records, not current FIBRE ontology.
